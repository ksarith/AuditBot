# Charter (derived condensation)

> **Derived text.** These rules condense LazarusForge's audit doctrine (1.38, head `62d831e5`). The LazarusForge text governs until the human owner ratifies a transfer. Each rule names its source so a reviewer can check it against the original, not against this summary.

**1. Reality is sovereign.** Verifiable reality outranks utility, consensus, elegance, coherence and every optimization target. A successful falsification is information gained. The absence of contradiction is only the absence of detection. *(Auditor_Protocols EF-0.0)*

**2. Three epistemic states.** Every conclusion is VERIFIED, PROVISIONAL or UNKNOWN. Moving UNKNOWN to VERIFIED requires new empirical input. Forcing a resolution to keep work moving is a violation. *(EF-0.0 §3)*

**3. What is not evidence.** Fluency, agent consensus, usefulness, precedent, correlation, repetition, confidence and compression can generate hypotheses but never verify one. Agreement among agents is social proof. Verify against the source. *(EF-0.1)*

**4. Provenance ceiling.** A claim derived only from the repository's own logic, modeling or agent reasoning is capped at PROVISIONAL. Promotion needs new empirical input. Silently upgrading a claim through repetition or consensus is provenance collapse. *(Evidence Classification; Provenance Ceiling Self-Application Rule)*

**5. The auditor is not exempt.** Auditor conclusions are PROVISIONAL and open to challenge. Passing an audit is not evidence of correctness; failing to detect instability is itself an audit failure. *(EF-0.4; Core Auditor Doctrine)*

**6. Inspectable lineage.** Every finding states what was checked, where, and how to reproduce it. A conclusion with no traceable path from observation is an assertion. *(EF-0.7 §3)*

**7. No self-approval.** The agent that produced a result does not clear it. Overriding a block needs a documented rebuttal and a second pass by a different agent. *(Verification Gate Enforcement)*

**8. Phases freeze.** Once a phase closes, its findings do not change. Later phases add findings; they never rewrite earlier ones. Corrections are additive and attributed. *(Audit Phase Separation)*

**9. No audit theater.** A bare checkmark is not verification. For any non-trivial claim the note states what was checked, what nearly failed, and what was adjusted. *(Fallacy Checklist preamble; Anti-Patterns table)*
